Every lap, a chapter

Drift Panel brings together the best tracks to turn every lap into an epic moment. Playlists built for adrenaline: intense beats for acceleration, distinctive grooves for controlled corners, and atmospheric tracks to warm up before hitting the track.

Browse, load your own music, and customize playlists without leaving your browser — everything runs locally, with nothing uploaded to any server.

Built for drivers and gamers

Simple playback controls, shuffle and repeat, with local storage for your playlists. Focus on driving or gaming — we've got the soundtrack covered.

Create custom sets for every kind of session: practice, races, sim racing, or just a free ride. Save combinations and switch modes with one click.
